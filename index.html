01 · Courses · In beta

Minute Studio

Guidance, turned into practice. A few minutes a day, and something of your own at the end.

Minute Studio is for things you want to be able to do, not just read about once. A course is a series of short sessions, a few minutes each, and every session makes or improves a piece of your own work: a drawing, a text, a plan, a value logged, an answer given.

The minutes you would give a feed: a feed never ends and leaves nothing behind. A session ends, and takes something of yours a step further.

  • Short sessions

    One unit a day

    One card at a time: a question before the explanation, an earlier one asked again, and it ends on something you did. The clock stops when you do.

  • Your folder

    You end with something to show

    Each session adds to your piece, beside graded examples and typical flaws to check it against. The folder keeps every version, first to newest.

  • For authors

    Write courses people follow

    Shape your own material, or the plan your assistant wrote, into short sessions in the studio. Publish to the catalogue, or keep a course for yourself.

02 · Checklists · iPhone, iPad and Android

Reverun

Better every time. Checklists you run again and again, and improve after each run.

Reverun is a beautifully simple app for the processes you repeat: a morning ritual, the end-of-workday shutdown, home maintenance, packing for a weekend trip. Write the steps once as a playbook, then start a run whenever you need it.

Each run keeps its own progress, notes and adjustments, without touching the playbook. When you finish, you decide which changes to carry forward, so the next run starts from what you learned.

  1. Create
  2. Run
  3. Complete
  4. Improve

  • Playbooks

    Write it down once

    Ordered steps, optional sections, photos where they help. Start a run on demand or on a simple recurring schedule.

  • Runs

    Adjust as you go

    Check steps off, take notes, attach a photo, adjust steps for this run only. Finished runs stay in your history exactly as they happened.

  • Carry forward

    Decide what's better next time

    At the end of a run, review what changed and pick what becomes part of the playbook. That's the whole idea.
