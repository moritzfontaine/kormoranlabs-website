Reverun · Support

How can we help?

Write to write@kormoranlabs.com. A person reads every message and answers it.

It helps to mention your device (iPhone, iPad or Android, and its system version) and the Reverun version, shown at the bottom of Settings. A screenshot is often the quickest way to show what you see.

Common questions

Where is my data, and how do I keep it safe?

Everything is stored on your device only; there is no account and no cloud copy. Export a backup now and then: Settings → Backup → Export backup, and keep the file somewhere else, such as Files, a cloud drive or an email to yourself.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, export a backup and save the file where the new phone can reach it. On the new phone, open Settings → Backup → Restore from a file and choose it. Restoring replaces what is on the new device; if you change your mind, Undo last restore puts the earlier data back.

I bought Reverun Full. How do I get it back after a reinstall or on a new device?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Reverun checks with the store when it starts and unlocks by itself. If it doesn't, open Settings → Reverun Full → Unlock Reverun Full and tap Restore purchase. A purchase that is waiting for approval (for example Ask to Buy) unlocks by itself once it goes through.

What does the free version include?

Two playbooks, archived ones included, with unlimited runs, notes and history. Reverun Full is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, that adds as many playbooks as you like. Nothing you have is ever locked: over the limit, everything keeps working, and only adding a new playbook asks for Reverun Full.

My reminders don't arrive.

Reminders come for playbooks that have a schedule, at the time you set. Check that notifications are allowed for Reverun in your device settings, and that the playbook isn't archived: archived playbooks can't be scheduled.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone and iPad, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, through your order history in Google Play. If something doesn't work as it should, write to us first: we'd like to fix it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the playbooks and runs you no longer want in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. We hold no copy. Details are in the privacy policy.

Ideas and feedback

Reverun gets better the way your playbooks do: one run at a time. If something could be better next time, tell us.
