Reverun · Privacy policy

Your data stays on your device.

Last updated 22 November 2026

Reverun keeps everything you put into it on your phone or tablet. There are no accounts, no servers for the app, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your playbooks, runs, notes, photos or videos.

Who we are

Reverun is made by Moritz Fontaine, France, under the name Kormoran Labs. He is responsible for the personal data described in this policy (the controller). For anything about this policy or your data, write to write@kormoranlabs.com.

What Reverun stores, and where

Everything you create in Reverun is saved in the app's own storage on your device, and nowhere else:

  • your playbooks, their steps, sections, versions and schedules;
  • your runs, with their progress, notes and adjustments, and your history;
  • photos and videos you attach to a step (Reverun keeps its own copy, so it still works if you delete the original);
  • your settings, and whether you have Reverun Full.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

If your device's own backup is switched on (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Google's backup on Android), the system may include Reverun's data in that backup, under Apple's or Google's terms. You control this in your device settings.

Permissions

Reverun only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it, and works without any of them.

  • Camera, photos and microphone: to attach a photo or video to a step. Only the items you take or choose are copied into the app. The microphone records sound only while you record a video.
  • Notifications: to remind you of a scheduled run. Reminders are planned on your device itself; no server sends them, and no push token or device identifier leaves your device.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings.

Backup files

When you export a backup, Reverun creates a file with your playbooks, runs, notes and settings, and hands it to your device's share sheet. You decide where it goes: Files, a cloud drive, an email. From then on it is handled by the service you chose, under its own terms. Restoring reads only the file you pick.

Purchases

Reverun Full is a one-time purchase made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies; we never receive your payment details. When it starts, the app asks the store whether your store account already owns Reverun Full, so that a purchase comes back after a reinstall or on a new device.

Apple and Google give us the sales reports they provide to every developer. We use them only for our accounts, and to help if you contact us about a purchase.

Analytics and crash reports

Reverun contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising software, and does not track you across apps or websites. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple or Google may show us aggregated statistics and crash reports about the app. These do not identify you.

Beta testing

If you test a pre-release version of Reverun through Apple's TestFlight or a Google Play testing track, Apple or Google run the test and give us some information about it, under their own terms:

  • the name and email address you were invited with, if we invited you by email or added you to a Google Play test list (if you joined through a public TestFlight link, Apple does not show us your email address);
  • when you installed the test version, how often it was opened, and crash reports, with your device model and system version;
  • the feedback you choose to send through TestFlight or Google Play, including any screenshot you attach.

The app itself still sends us nothing: your playbooks, runs, notes, photos and videos stay on your device during a test too. We use this information only to run the test, fix problems and improve Reverun, and delete what we keep of it once the test has ended, or earlier if you ask. You can leave a test at any time in TestFlight or Google Play. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in testing the app before its release (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Links

A link you add to a step opens in your browser when you tap it. Reverun does not load or preview it itself.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and your message only to answer you. When you tap Send feedback in the app's settings, Reverun opens your own mail app with a message to us that names the Reverun version and your device's system (for example iOS 26.1); you see everything in it, can change or delete it, and decide whether to send it. We keep the conversation as long as it is needed for that, and delete it when you ask. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering the messages we receive (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Children

Reverun collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Because your Reverun data is only on your device, you exercise most of these rights directly in the app: edit, delete or export what you like. For anything else, such as an email you sent us, write to write@kormoranlabs.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or the authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If Reverun ever starts handling your data differently, for example through optional sync between devices, we will update this policy before that version is released.
