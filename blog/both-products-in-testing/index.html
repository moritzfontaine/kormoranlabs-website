Two products, both in testing

Minute Studio and Reverun are in their first test. What they are for, and why now is the best time to tell us what you think.

Kormoran Labs makes tools for getting better, a little at a time. Like the cormorant, we go deep: into how people learn, practise and improve. Then we surface with tools for it. Today there are two, and both are in testing.

Minute Studio

Short courses, a few minutes a day. Each session makes or improves something of your own: a drawing, a text, a plan. A question comes first, then the why, and every session ends on something you did. Your folder keeps every version of your work, first to newest.

Authors can turn their own material into short sessions, and publish it or keep it for themselves. Unit 1 of every course is free, no account needed: minute-studio.com.

Reverun

Checklists for the things you repeat: a morning ritual, a workday shutdown, packing for a trip. You write a playbook once, run it when you need it, adjust as you go, and at the end choose which changes the playbook keeps. Create, run, complete, improve.

Reverun works offline, needs no account and keeps your data on your device. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, and goes to the App Store and Google Play after the test.

Why your feedback matters now

Both products are still taking shape. Tell us what works, what doesn't and what's missing: it goes straight into the next version. In Minute Studio, the Feedback button in the app works too.
